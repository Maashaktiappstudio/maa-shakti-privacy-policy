Privacy Policy

Maa Shakti App Studio (All Web & Android Applications)
Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Maa Shakti App Studio ("we," "our," or "us") builds utility, accounting, and business management web and mobile applications (including HajriBook and other tools). This Privacy Policy explains how our applications collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use in our apps, we may request access to limited information strictly necessary for app functionality:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth, we access your basic profile details (such as your Name and Email Address) solely to identify your account within the app.
  • Google Drive & Google Sheets Access: For apps featuring cloud backup or accounting sync, the app creates and updates spreadsheets/files exclusively inside your own Google Drive. We do not access, read, or delete your other personal Drive files.
  • Location Data (Optional): Certain attendance or field-work apps may request GPS location permission solely to verify workplace check-ins when you actively mark attendance. Location is not tracked in the background.

2. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • User-Controlled Storage: Your business records, attendance logs, and financial entries are stored locally on your device or directly inside your personal Google Drive/Sheets account.
  • No External Selling: Maa Shakti App Studio does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or business records to any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Our applications may integrate trusted third-party services to operate smoothly and remain free for users:

  • Google API Services: Used for authentication and Google Sheets/Drive synchronization. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Advertising Partners: Free versions of our web or mobile apps may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Adsterra, or Monetag), which may use standard cookies or device identifiers to show relevant ads.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can revoke our app's access to your Google Account anytime from your Google Account Security Permissions page.
  • You can delete your app data anytime by clearing your browser/app storage or deleting the generated spreadsheet directly from your Google Drive.

5. Children's Privacy

Our business and utility applications are intended for general and professional use and are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we launch new applications or add features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
